## 4. History

- Lillian developed as a small rural farming community in Johnson County, organized enough at one point to support its own independent school district.
- The Lillian Independent School District served the community directly for decades before merging into the Alvarado Independent School District on July 1, 1986 — a common pattern among small rural Texas districts consolidating with larger neighboring systems.
- Lillian Elementary School has continued to operate within Alvarado ISD, preserving the community's educational identity even after the merger.
- The community has remained unincorporated and very small throughout its history, with its current population (around 59 within the ZIP code area) reflecting a long-standing rural, low-density character rather than any recent decline or growth surge.
