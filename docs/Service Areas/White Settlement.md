# White Settlement, TX — Service Area Research Profile

## 1. City Overview

- **County**: Tarrant County
- **Location relative to Kingdom Care Roofing & Construction**: Approximately 17–22 miles north of Kingdom Care's Burleson, TX base (sources vary slightly)
- **Nearest major roads/highways**: White Settlement Road, near Lockheed Martin/Naval Air Station Fort Worth JRB and the western edge of Fort Worth
- **Population**: 18,084 (2021 estimate), up from 16,116 at the 2010 census; grew explosively from roughly 500 to over 10,000 residents during the World War II era, driven by military aircraft manufacturing demand
- **Incorporation**: Community origins trace to the 1850s, shortly after Fort Worth's founding (November 1849) and the creation of Tarrant County; officially incorporated as a city on May 24, 1941
- **General character**: A northwestern Fort Worth suburb with deep historical roots tied to both early Texas settlement and the WWII-era aviation industry boom that gave the city its modern population base.

## 3. Local Landmarks & Points of Interest

- **Schools**: White Settlement Independent School District — serves White Settlement and a portion of west central Fort Worth. Trinity Academy was among the area's earliest schools, historically serving as both a schoolhouse and an important community meeting place near present-day Cherry Lane.
- **Historic/Cultural**: White Settlement Historical Museum, 8320 Hanon Drive, depicting the lives of early American settlers; First Baptist Church of White Settlement, 7801 White Settlement Rd., a significant institution in Tarrant County religious history since 1954.
- **Name origin**: The city's name reflects its 1840s–1850s history as an area of White settler homesteads amid what was then predominantly American Indian territory.

## 4. History

- White Settlement's community origins date to the 1850s, shortly after the establishment of Fort Worth in November 1849 and the creation of Tarrant County, as early American settlers established homesteads in an area previously populated predominantly by American Indian villages — a history reflected directly in the community's name.
- The first area census in 1850 recorded 599 White American residents and 65 Black enslaved people.
- The City of White Settlement officially incorporated on May 24, 1941, just as World War II's demand for military aircraft manufacturing was about to transform the local economy.
- Spurred by wartime aviation manufacturing (nearby defense production facilities), the city's population surged from roughly 500 to over 10,000 residents in a remarkably short period.
- Growth has continued at a more modest pace since, reaching 16,116 by the 2010 Census and an estimated 18,084 by 2021.

## 5. Weather & Storm Patterns

- **Climate baseline**: Humid subtropical climate consistent with the greater DFW metro — hot summers, mild winters, active spring/early-summer severe weather season.
- **Hail risk**: Tarrant County is consistently ranked among the top Texas counties for wind and hail insurance claims (2007–2017 data); White Settlement shares full exposure to the DFW hail corridor, including the record 2016 North Texas hail season (~500,000 statewide claims, $4+ billion in insured losses).
- **Tornado risk**: The DFW metro has recorded roughly 350 tornadoes since 1952; the December 26, 2015 Garland–Rowlett EF4 outbreak ($1.55 billion in damage) remains the costliest tornado event in metro history.
- **Wind**: White Settlement's mature, WWII-era and postwar housing stock typically carries established tree canopy, adding debris-related roof damage risk during high-wind events.
- **Roofing material impact**: Given the city's largely mature, built-out housing stock, the market here skews heavily toward full tear-off reroof and Class 4 impact-resistant upgrade conversations rather than new-construction specification.
