# Benbrook, TX — Service Area Research Profile

## 1. City Overview

- **County**: Tarrant County
- **Location relative to Kingdom Care Roofing & Construction**: Approximately 15 miles northwest of Kingdom Care's Burleson, TX base
- **Nearest major roads/highways**: U.S. Highway 377, Interstate 20, State Highway 183
- **Population**: Approximately 24,500+ (2020 Census: 24,520); grew steadily from 161 residents (1935) to 8,169 (1970), 13,579 (1980), 19,564 (1990), 20,208 (2000), and 21,234 (2010) — slow, consistent suburban growth over nine decades
- **Incorporation**: Incorporated in 1948 (celebrated its 50th anniversary of incorporation in November 1997)
- **General character**: A mature, established suburb in southwest Tarrant County adjacent to Fort Worth, built around Benbrook Lake — a mix of long-settled mid-century neighborhoods and lake-adjacent residential areas with a relaxed, small-town feel despite its proximity to Fort Worth.

---

## 2. Neighborhoods and Subdivisions

- **Benbrook Lakeside**: Lake-adjacent neighborhood with a mix of housing eras; waterfront exposure adds wind/moisture stress relevant to roofing lifespan.
- **Benbrook North**: Well-established area with convenient access to Fort Worth and surrounding Tarrant County; housing stock skews mid-century (1960s–1980s), a strong zone for full reroof and architectural-shingle upgrade conversations.
- **Older in-town areas near historic Benbrook Station/Mary's Creek**: Tied to the city's 1880s railroad-era origin; some of the oldest housing stock in the city.
- **Newer residential pockets near US-377 and city edges**: More recent construction with architectural shingle more standard from the builder.

---

## 3. Local Landmarks & Points of Interest

- **Schools**: Fort Worth Independent School District serves Benbrook, including Benbrook Elementary and Westpark Elementary (located within city limits), plus Waverly Park Elementary and Ridglea Hills Elementary (Fort Worth) serving portions of the city. Benbrook Middle School opened in 2011, and a combined middle/high school facility opened in 2014 funded by a 2007 FWISD bond.
- **Recreation**: Benbrook Lake (formed 1952 after construction of Benbrook Dam, built starting May 1947 by the U.S. Army Corps of Engineers) — a major recreational draw with boating, fishing, and park access.
- **Historic sites**: The self-guided Benbrook History Tour features 16 historic sites throughout the community, including locations along Mary's Creek tied to early settlement, historic schools, and other landmarks.
- **Civic anchor**: Benbrook Area Chamber of Commerce.

---

## 4. History

- Settlement in the Benbrook area dates to the mid-1800s, when farms and small communities developed on what was then the western frontier of Fort Worth.
- In 1880, the community successfully petitioned the Texas and Pacific Railroad to establish a station west of Fort Worth; the resulting "Benbrook Station" gave the growing settlement its name.
- The city incorporated in 1948, formalizing decades of gradual settlement.
- Benbrook Dam construction began in May 1947, and Benbrook Lake was formed in 1952, becoming the city's defining geographic and recreational feature and a draw for residential growth along its shoreline.
- Population growth was steady through the late 20th century (8,169 in 1970 to over 20,000 by 2000), with continued incremental growth into the 2020s as part of the broader southwest Fort Worth/Tarrant County suburban expansion.

## 5. Weather & Storm Patterns

- **Climate baseline**: Humid subtropical climate consistent with the greater DFW/North Texas region — hot summers, mild winters, active spring severe weather season.
- **Hail risk**: Tarrant County is consistently ranked among the top Texas counties for wind and hail insurance claims (2007–2017 data), and Benbrook shares full exposure to the DFW hail corridor, including the record 2016 North Texas hail season (~500,000 statewide claims, $4+ billion in insured losses).
- **Tornado risk**: The DFW metro has recorded roughly 350 tornadoes since 1952, with the December 26, 2015 outbreak (Garland–Rowlett EF4, $1.55 billion in damage) standing as the costliest tornado event in metro history.
- **Wind**: Lake-adjacent exposure in Benbrook Lakeside can see elevated straight-line wind effects off the open water, compounding shingle-lift risk during spring storm season.
- **Roofing material impact**: The combination of regional hail intensity and lake-adjacent wind exposure makes impact-rated shingle upgrades a relevant conversation for Benbrook homeowners, particularly in lakefront-facing properties.

## 6. Roofing & Construction Industry Relevance

- **Materials by era**: Older housing near the historic Benbrook Station/Mary's Creek core and the 1960s–1980s Benbrook North stock are largely on multiple generations of 3-tab asphalt shingle replacement. Lake-adjacent and newer residential pockets near US-377 trend toward architectural (dimensional) shingle, with Class 4 impact-resistant options increasingly relevant at next replacement.
- **Replacement/upgrade sweet spot**: With a large share of housing stock dating to the 1960s–1990s boom, Benbrook has a deep base of homes in the 25–50+ year range — strong candidates for full tear-off and reroof, especially where original or early-generation shingles remain.
- **Insurance claim patterns**: Tarrant County's top-ten ranking for wind/hail claims applies directly to Benbrook; claim-driven replacement activity follows the same regional storm-event cadence as the rest of the DFW metro.
- **HOA considerations**: Benbrook has a mix of non-HOA legacy neighborhoods and some newer HOA-governed subdivisions; verify CC&Rs for architectural review requirements on shingle type/color before quoting upgrades in newer pockets.
- **Permit authority**: City of Benbrook Permit and Inspection Department, located at Benbrook City Hall, 911 Winscott Road, Benbrook, TX 76126 — phone **(817) 249-6064**. Office hours Monday–Friday, 8 a.m.–5 p.m.
- **Code requirements**: Texas has no mandatory statewide residential building code; the state adopted the IRC as a default standard, but each incorporated city enforces its own adopted edition and permitting process. Benbrook, as an incorporated city, administers its own building permit and inspection requirements — confirm current code edition with the Permit and Inspection Department before scoping work.
