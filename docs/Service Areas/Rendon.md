# Rendon, TX — Service Area Research Profile

## 1. City Overview

- **County**: Tarrant County
- **Location relative to Kingdom Care Roofing & Construction**: Approximately 10 miles northeast of Kingdom Care's Burleson, TX base, situated along FM 1187 between Burleson/Crowley and Mansfield; roughly 20 miles southeast of downtown Fort Worth
- **Nearest major roads/highways**: Farm to Market Road 1187 (runs through Rendon connecting Burleson/Crowley to Mansfield/US 287), Rendon Road, Risinger Road
- **Population**: 13,533 as of the 2020 Census; grew from 7,658 (1990) to 9,022 (2000), 12,552 (2010), and 13,533 (2020) — steady, sustained growth
- **Incorporation**: Rendon is unincorporated — a Census-Designated Place (CDP) in Tarrant County rather than an incorporated municipality, meaning it has no independent city government
- **General character**: A fast-growing unincorporated semi-rural-to-suburban community southeast of Fort Worth, wedged between Burleson, Crowley, and Mansfield, transitioning from acreage/rural lots to newer suburban-style subdivisions as DFW growth pushes outward.

## 4. History

- Rendon developed as a rural farming community in Tarrant County along what became FM 1187, which was originally designated on July 14, 1949, running east from US 81 roughly 5.1 miles to Rendon before later extensions connected it westward through Crowley toward US 377.
- As an unincorporated area, Rendon never pursued municipal incorporation, instead remaining under Tarrant County governance while nearby Burleson, Crowley, and Mansfield incorporated and grew around it.
- Population held in the single-digit thousands through the late 20th century (7,658 in 1990) before accelerating in the 2000s and 2010s (9,022 in 2000, 12,552 in 2010, 13,533 in 2020), driven by the broader DFW suburban expansion along the FM 1187 corridor.
- The area continues to see residential growth pressure from adjacent incorporated cities, with new subdivision development increasingly blurring the line between Rendon and neighboring Crowley/Burleson suburban growth.

## 5. Weather & Storm Patterns

- **Climate baseline**: Humid subtropical climate typical of the DFW metro — hot summers, mild winters, active spring/early-summer severe weather season.
- **Hail risk**: Tarrant County is a core part of the DFW hail corridor, fully exposed to major regional events including the record-setting 2016 North Texas hail season (~500,000 statewide claims, $4+ billion in insured losses).
- **Tornado risk**: The DFW metro has recorded roughly 350 tornadoes since 1952; Rendon's position in the Fort Worth–Burleson–Mansfield triangle places it within the same spring severe-weather risk band as the rest of the metro.
- **Wind**: Rendon's more open, semi-rural terrain along FM 1187 offers less windbreak than denser suburban areas, meaning straight-line thunderstorm winds can have a more direct impact on roofing than in tree-covered, built-up neighborhoods.
- **Roofing material impact**: The combination of older rural homes with minimal wind protection and full regional hail exposure makes Rendon a strong candidate market for Class 4 impact-resistant shingle upgrades, both on legacy rural homes and newer suburban-style construction.
