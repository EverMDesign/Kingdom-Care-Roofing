- Covington was founded in the spring of 1852 by Colonel James J. Gathings, who acquired approximately 10,000 acres in what would become Hill County, setting aside 100 acres specifically for the new town.
- The town has remained small and stable throughout its history, never growing into a significant population center even as Hill County and the broader DFW region urbanized around it.

- **Tornado risk**: The DFW/North Texas region has recorded roughly 350 tornadoes since 1952 in the immediate metro, with rural Hill County sharing the same general spring severe-weather risk window as the rest of North Texas, including the historically significant December 26, 2015 outbreak period.
- **Wind**: Open rural/agricultural terrain around Covington offers little windbreak, meaning straight-line thunderstorm winds can have a more direct impact on roofing than in denser, tree-covered suburban settings.
