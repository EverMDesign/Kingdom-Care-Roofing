## 5. Weather & Storm Patterns

- **Climate baseline**: Humid subtropical climate shared with the rest of Johnson County and the DFW metro — hot summers, mild winters, and a spring/early-summer severe weather season.
- **Hail risk**: Johnson County is consistently ranked among the top Texas counties for wind and hail insurance claims (2007–2017 data), and Briaroaks' immediate adjacency to Burleson means it shares full exposure to the same regional hail corridor, including the record 2016 North Texas hail season (~500,000 statewide claims, $4+ billion in insured losses).
- **Tornado risk**: The DFW metro has recorded roughly 350 tornadoes since 1952, with the December 26, 2015 outbreak (Garland–Rowlett EF4) standing as the costliest tornado event in metro history — the same regional risk window applies to Briaroaks.
- **Wind**: Open acreage lots common in Briaroaks offer less windbreak from surrounding structures/tree cover than denser suburban lots, which can mean greater direct wind exposure during straight-line wind events.
- **Roofing material impact**: Given rural acreage exposure and full DFW hail-corridor risk, Class 4 impact-resistant shingle upgrades are a strong fit for Briaroaks homeowners, particularly on open, less-sheltered lots.
